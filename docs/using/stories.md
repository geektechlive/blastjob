# Interview stories

**What this covers:** How to keep your interview stories in the cockpit and what the number check under each one means. It is for anyone using the cockpit. For how it works inside, see [story bank](../how-it-works/stories.md).

## Why keep them here

A story told two ways in two interviews, with a different number each time, costs you. Write each story once, in your own words, and keep it here to rehearse from.

blastjob does not use these stories anywhere else. It does not draft them, and the prep pack and packets do not read them. They are yours.

## Add a story

Open **Stories**, under **More** then **Research**. Give it a title (80 characters at most) and write at least one of situation, task, action and result (1,500 characters each). You can add the employer, a four-digit year and tags such as "cost savings, vendor management". Press **Save story**.

## Edit or delete

Each story has an **Edit** section and a **Delete** button. If you opened the story in two tabs and saved in one, the other tab's save is refused and says so; the newer text stays, and what you typed is kept in the edit box so you can copy it. The same goes for delete.

## The number check

Under a story, "Not in your work history: 95%" lists numbers in the story that are not in your work history. It compares whole figures, so `$1,200` is not matched by `$1,200,000`. It is a prompt to check the number before you say it in an interview. It never stops you saving.
