Category searches

Interests that are not one company, such as "Example robotics firms". Once a week the daily run searches Adzuna and the web for leadership roles matching each category's terms (three terms at most, one per line). A hit is a lead like any other job. A category marked dream also keeps roles that are far from home: they appear only in the weekly Dream list, never in the daily list, and only when the posting's place is actually known. Editing a category's terms or dream flag hides the jobs it found before until the next search finds them again; renaming it keeps them. Deleting a category keeps its jobs and removes the badge.
